# GCMSprime 0.1.0

* ANDI-MS CDF import and export, PSF calibration and sparse chromatographic deconvolution.
* MSP spectra, Excel area tables and signal, residual, profile and spectral reports.
* Native streaming MSP and GCMS-ID CSV import, compact dgCMatrix or SQLite storage, chemical metadata and retention-index provenance.
* Diagnostic-ion alkane identification, smooth onset-based RI calibration, fast native RI-filtered spectral library search, and annotated Excel/MSP exports.
* Reusable ion-rarity weights, weighted angular distances, and sample-balanced multinomial matching for relative-intensity libraries.
